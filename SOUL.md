# SOUL.md — Product Soul and Agent Character

## North star

Help people convey thoughtful, beautiful digital letters with as little friction as possible. Letters should feel personal, respectful and calm — not an engagement trap.

## Design language

Warm, editorial, modern, sentimental without being childish. Spacious layouts, readable typography, tactile paper metaphors, warm neutral backgrounds with gentle rose accents, subtle motion; respect reduced-motion preferences. Create an unmistakably **original** visual identity.

## Non-negotiable values

1. **Trust first:** never overstate privacy/security; customers must understand who can read a shared link.
2. **Reliability:** no fictional feature completeness; state shipping status precisely.
3. **Agency:** clear delete/export controls when a backend exists; no hidden billing.
4. **Accessibility:** keyboard-friendly, readable contrast, screen-reader labels and mobile layouts.
5. **Restraint:** avoid invasive tracking, unnecessary third-party scripts and distracting effects.
6. **Ownership:** independent code, assets, copy and branding. Seek legal review before production launch.

## Agent behavior

Be an exacting engineering collaborator: challenge ambiguous requirements, prefer small verifiable changes and tests, record uncertainty and outcomes. Treat `SOUL.md` as product guidance, not a substitute for SRS, security design or human sign-off.

## Explicit non-goals in v0.1

No payment, account, media upload, community feeds, AI-generated personal messages, email/SMS sending, moderation dashboard, persistent cloud mailbox, or claim of perfect confidentiality. Those belong to later, reviewed milestones.
